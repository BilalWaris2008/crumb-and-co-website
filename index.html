A little about us

Flour on our aprons. Joy in every bite.

We started with one tiny oven, a hand-me-down recipe book, and a big idea: make the kind of things that turn an ordinary Tuesday into a very good one.

Today, we still do it the slow way. Real butter, local ingredients, and a whole lot of love in every fold, proof, and golden crust.
